ReconHurricane-hunter missions live, and every 2026 flight archived: flight-level winds, dropsondes, Doppler radar. Global ArchiveEvery tropical cyclone since 1842: tracks, intensity, infrared imagery and side-by-side comparison. Global IR ArchiveReplay any season since 1980 over near-global infrared imagery, with every storm’s track. TC-RADAR1,510 airborne Doppler radar analyses from 1997–2024, with 3D winds and cross-sections. TC-RADAR CompositesComposite radar structure across storms, filtered by intensity, shear, tilt and motion. TC ClimatologyBasin statistics, an ERA5 reanalysis globe, and MJO and BSISO phase composites.
